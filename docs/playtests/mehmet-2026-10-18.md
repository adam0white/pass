# Playtest: Mehmet, 2026-10-18

Late source-unread playtest of commit fd35428 on a 360x780 mobile context (Playwright). Persona: Mehmet, 41, ran a restaurant in Izmir for nine years. Model: Fable (the build and consults used Opus and Sonnet). His words are recorded verbatim below.

## What changed because of it

- The evening page states the pace the bank needs against what a full house would bring.
- The cooks' evening lines vary by cook and night; Dilek has her own.
- The crew row stays in the cook sheet, so you can switch cooks with a card open.
- Speeds are slower (steady is about 3 minutes a night) and bubbles expire with sim time.
- The hold-to-insist fill covers the whole button.
- Tickets read "grill ●○ / cold ●" with a word for the wait instead of symbols.
- The jar choice gets an immediate answer; the prayer line and first-night intro no longer repeat.
- Deferred (docs/design.md): refusals said at the pass, three cooks out back at once.

## His report

**Mehmet, 41, Izmir. Verbatim.**

**1. First 60 seconds.** I understood the setup fast: dead owner, two cooks who still think it's her place, bank wants ₺5,200 by Saturday. Good. Then the doors opened and nothing happened for twenty game-minutes. I tapped Selim and got six buttons that all said "yes". Yes to what? There were no tickets. I confused myself twice: "steady" is already fast, "fast" burned an hour of service in ten real seconds, and on Monday I lost a table before I understood the rail. Also: tapping Dilek's name while Selim's card is open does nothing, because the name row disappears. I thought the game froze.

**2. People or dice?** Mostly people, and that's the one thing this game has. Selim leaving the grill for maghrib every night at the same time, telling me "I'll still have enough for the rest of the rush" and later "I can do it, though the rest of the rush will be hard" — that's a real cook managing himself. When I held the button on him in the storeroom: "No. Not at the cost of maghrib." Correct. Then at the close: "You pushed me once tonight. I'll remember it." I believed him. Rafi's "I didn't keep my rule about the jar. Next time I'll be ready when it comes" is the best line in the game. What felt wrong: nobody ever refuses when the rail is full and they're just out back smoking; it's "Alright, I'll do it" or "Fine." every single time. Three people out back together at 20:48 Tuesday with nobody minding the pass. In nine years that happened to me zero times. And "A long night. You were there for it" is said by everyone, every night, in the same words. Dilek said "You left me to it. Nevin did that too." on Monday and nothing since; she's a ghost.

**3. Sharpest choice.** Pulling Selim off his meal and later off maghrib on Wednesday for a restless table. Consequence: he said no once, came back on his own, and at close told me he'd remember it. I also saw two tables walk while he prayed. That's the only loop in the game that actually closes. The jar choice I just made has no immediate response at all.

**4. Bugs and layout.** Duplicate log line Monday ("stepped into the storeroom for maghrib" twice). Speech bubbles ("Alright, I'll do it.", "Fine.") stay glued to the figures for an hour. Ticket glyphs △○● at that size are unreadable on a phone; I never knew which cold item was late. The ticket card status ("restless") is pale grey on beige. The kitchen drawing eats half the screen; the cook buttons and log sit below the fold once a card opens. The first-night story text repeats under the kitchen. Hold-to-insist has no visible timer or feedback.

**5. Demands, ranked.**
1. Show me the money math. ₺40 a table, ~20 tables, ₺5,200 by Saturday: I was dead on Monday night and nobody told me. Either tell me, or make tips/prices a lever.
2. Give me a reason to choose. Insist costs me something visible (trust bar, a look, a line), and refusal has to happen at the pass, not just in the storeroom.
3. Fix the controls: switch cooks with a card open, hold feedback, and make "steady" actually steady.
4. Readable tickets: words ("kebab late"), not dots.
5. Dilek needs a story. She has one line and a cigarette.

**6. Verdict.** I'd open Thursday to see what Selim remembers and what Rafi does with the jar, but I'd be playing for the people, not the kitchen, because the kitchen already told me I've lost.
