## Verdict: needs a fix cycle (0 keep, 9 mixed, 5 quit)

Every tester liked that the cooks answer with reasons and can refuse. Most return sentences carry one of two conditions: show what the People offers do, and show what my choices change. Five testers said no or not tonight (Nia, Jonas, Tessa, Pavel, Ruben).

### New blocking asks

1. **F-1953. A taken People offer shows no answer.** Maya: "I tapped an offer in People and the sheet closed with no visible answer." Aisha: "Three times I asked for a private talk (Dilek why she records, Selim Nevins notes, Rafi repair deposit) and nothing came back." Leila: "the offers I accepted vanished without a word". Jonas, Pavel, Emre and Samir said the same.
   - Confirmed: in 9 sessions the offer tap has `error: null`, so the game took it. Some answers appear later under "Earlier conversations"; testers did not see them at the time.
   - **Proposed fix:** when an offer is taken, open that conversation at once, or say on the spot when it will happen ("Dilek will tell you after the doors open").
2. **F-1954. Greyed People offers give no reason.** Nia: "The mystery button is greyed out. Why? Nothing tells me." Rosa: "the two offers are grey and I cannot tap them, no reason given." Diego, Emre, Mei, Tessa, Pavel and Ines said the same.
   - Confirmed: the driver answered "is shown but disabled right now (game state)" in 8 sessions.
   - **Proposed fix:** print the reason under each disabled offer ("Not during service. Ask after closing.").
3. **F-1955. Choices do not visibly change the money.** Pavel: "after the same totals came out on both devices (600 on Monday, 800 on Tuesday, whatever I did) I could not see what my choices change". Leila: "Monday came out the same (15 of 15 tables, 600 lira) whatever I did with the break". Emre, Diego, Samir, Ines and Aisha said the same.
   - Confirmed in the reports: Monday 600 and Tuesday 800 recur across testers with different plans. Whether plans can change these totals is not checked here.
   - **Proposed fix:** the closing summary names what the night's choices changed (tables lost or kept, lira lost to empty minutes), or says plainly that the takings depend on the tables booked. Do not lower the target.
4. **F-1956. The first stretch of service has nothing to do.** Nia: "Still no orders, time is flying and money rises by itself. I do not know what I am supposed to do. I am a boss with nothing to boss." Ruben: "two nights of 35 of 35 tables served with almost nothing to decide gave me nothing to trace back to one call." Diego, Maya, Jonas and Rosa said the same.
   - Confirmed: many sessions show "No orders are waiting" while money rises. This matches the pmg-followups finding that only 4 of 12 seeds ask anything before 20:00.
   - **Proposed fix:** build the night-one founder request proposed in pmg-followups (pass@17, one seeded decision at about 18:30), and show each served order as it earns.
5. **F-1957. The slowed clock leaves the player waiting with no move.** Emre: "I do not want to sit through slowed-time waits with nothing to do." Samir: "Three nights the same 80-second crawl ended with nobody willing to cover." Jonas: "time is crawling (one game minute per 10 real seconds) because the cold side is empty and I have no real way to fill it". Pavel said the same.
   - This is a pacing complaint, not a too-slow-to-tap complaint, so ruling 5 does not apply. Several testers measured it in real seconds.
   - **Proposed fix:** when every cook has refused or nobody is free, say so and return the clock to normal speed until something changes.
6. **F-1958. Cooks give the same stock lines.** Maya: "Both cooks answer How are you doing with the same line." Aisha: "Rafi gave the exact same sentence as Dilek". Tessa: "Selim and Rafi refused with the identical sentence". Nia, Samir and Leila said the same.
   - **Proposed fix:** give each cook their own replies to "How are you doing?" and "About what you said", drawn from what they told you in their notes.
7. **F-1959. Changing one break changes the cover by itself.** Emre: "changing a break time silently picks Rafi as the cover, and that would leave dishes empty." Jonas: "Dilek still says You will ask Rafi to take over, which I did not set." Aisha and Rosa said the same.
   - Seen by 4 testers on both devices; Emre first blamed a stray tap, then saw it again on the phone.
   - **Proposed fix:** keep the earlier cover choice when only the time changes, and never change another cook's plan unasked.
